Your blockchain transaction receipt will show a NOWPayments wallet address. This is expected behavior. NOWPayments is our secure, regulated crypto payment gateway — all orders are fulfilled directly by Phoenix Chems.

Why Crypto Only?

Traditional card networks (Visa, Mastercard) and most digital wallets have policies that prohibit or restrict transactions for peptide products due to regulatory classification in certain jurisdictions. This is an industry-wide reality that affects most research compound vendors.

By using cryptocurrency, we ensure that your order is processed quickly, privately, and without the risk of payment reversals or account freezes. Cryptocurrency payments are final, secure, and globally accessible — making them the ideal solution for research compound purchases.
