# Phoenics Peptide — Brand & Identity Guide

## 1. Brand Identity Overview
**Company Name:** Phoenics Peptide  
**Domain:** `phoenicspeptide.com`  
**Tagline:** Precision Research. Proven Purity.  
**Core Mission:** Providing synthesis-grade, ultra-pure peptide compounds for laboratory research, universities, biotech institutions, and analytical sciences globally across North America, Europe, Canada, Africa, and Asia.

---

## 2. Logo Design & Symbolism
The Phoenics Peptide brand insignia combines:
1. **The Mythic Phoenix:** Symbolizing renewal, cellular vitality, longevity, and transformation.
2. **Double Helix DNA / Peptide Backbone:** Embedded within the center of the bird's torso and wings, highlighting biomolecular engineering, purity, and analytical precision.
3. **Gold Core Crystal & Peptide Bonds:** Representing gold-standard third-party lab verification (HPLC & MS >99% purity).

## 4. Typography
- **Display & Headings:** `Montserrat` (Weights: 600 SemiBold, 700 Bold, 800 ExtraBold) — modern, scientific authority.
- **Body & Product Content:** `Inter` (Weights: 400 Regular, 500 Medium, 600 SemiBold) — ultra-readable cross-platform interface typography.
- **Technical & Batch Specifications:** `Space Mono` or `Courier New` — for CAS numbers, sequence strings, molecular formulas, and lot numbers.

## 5. Regulatory & Compliance Notice
Every page, product listing, invoice, and email footer MUST prominently feature the statutory compliance statement:
> *"All products sold on phoenicspeptide.com are intended strictly for in-vitro research and laboratory analytical use only. Not for human consumption, cosmetic, or clinical diagnostic use."*
